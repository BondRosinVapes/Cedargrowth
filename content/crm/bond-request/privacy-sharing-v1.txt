If you tick the box to send a product request from your Haus page, we pass your first name, the one contact you choose, the dispensary you picked and the Bond products and quantities you asked for to the Cedargrowth LLC customer system, which emails that dispensary only so it can reply to you about that request. We send nothing else about you, the customer system does not use it for marketing, and it deletes your name and contact 7 days after the email goes out, and in any case no later than 14 days after you send the request. Our email service, Resend, keeps a copy of that email for up to 30 days. The dispensary confirms availability and price, takes payment in store, and keeps its copy of the email under its own privacy practices.
